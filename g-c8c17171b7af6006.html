HOW SAVING WORKS
Saving is automatic. Every change is saved online under this game's code and on this laptop.
To come back: on this laptop, sign in as instructor and the game opens where you left off. On any other computer, sign in as instructor and enter the game code under Resume a game. Wings rejoin with the same code.
