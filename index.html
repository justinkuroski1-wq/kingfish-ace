What it does

How a session runs

01 · 10 MINUTES

Set up one laptop

Sign in as instructor and open the projector window. Wings join from their own computers with the game code, or in windows on the same laptop.

02 · 30 MINUTES

Plan

Wings choose three spokes, roll aircraft and RFIs, load chalks and plot their first routes.

03 · 12 TURNS

Play and debrief

Each 30-minute turn is an 8-hour shift. Aircraft fly, tasks progress, Red attacks in a conflict, and the game ends in a debrief.
